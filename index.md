Hi, nice to meet you — welcome to my site!

My name is **Coco (Qingyang) Liu**, and I am a master’s student of **Biostatistics at Duke University**. I received my bachelor’s degree from the **University of Washington**, where I studied **Data Science and Statistics** with a strong foundation in applied mathematics and computation.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **statistical modeling, machine learning, and biomedical data**, with a particular focus on **single-cell genomics, rare disease gene discovery, and interpretable machine learning**. I am particularly interested in developing methods that integrate complex biological data with statistical and computational approaches to support scientific discovery and healthcare applications.

Outside of research, I enjoy photography, **music, and travel. I play the piano, especially enjoy the works of Frédéric Chopin, and like capturing moments and landscapes through photography. I also love traveling and experiencing new places and cultures, which continually inspires my curiosity and creativity.
